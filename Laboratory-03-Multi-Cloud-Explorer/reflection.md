# Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong focus on artificial intelligence, data analytics, and Kubernetes. These capabilities make it useful for organizations working with modern applications and large amounts of data. I also found its cloud services useful for understanding how different workloads can be deployed and managed.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have several similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and other cloud services. They also support scalable infrastructure, allowing organizations to increase or decrease resources depending on their requirements.

Business requirements play an important role in selecting a cloud platform. Factors such as budget, existing technology, security, workload type, scalability, and location can affect the decision. For example, an organization using Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an AI-focused organization may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that selecting a cloud provider is not only about choosing the most popular platform. The solution should match the actual needs and goals of the organization. Comparing services and business scenarios made it easier to understand why different companies may choose different cloud providers.

Maintaining the GitHub portfolio also improved my ability to organize technical information. Using separate Markdown files for research, comparisons, recommendations, and reflection made the work easier to manage and review. It also provided a record of the activities completed throughout the laboratory.
